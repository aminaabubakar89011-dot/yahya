Our Mission

Building practical support for resilient communities.

Who we are

Yahya Aid is a community support initiative focused on helping women, families, and home-based workers access the resources they need to become more stable and self-sufficient.

We work with local partners, community leaders, and volunteers to create an inclusive system of support that values dignity, care, and practical action.

Our values

  • Compassion and dignity
  • Transparency in assistance
  • Women-led empowerment
  • Community-centered support
